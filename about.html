About The Society for the Works of Magnús Pálsson (FUHMP)

The Society for the Works of Magnús Pálsson (FUHMP - Félag um höfundarverk Magnúsar Pálssonar) was founded by family and friends of artist Magnús Pálsson to make it possible to research and document the unique and comprehensive body of work of Magnús. Magnús is one of the pioneers of contemporary art in Iceland and has worked in most fields of creative media for more than 70 years. The organization does not engage in commercial activities and is not intended to generate profit.
